About this event

Turn your ideas into working solutions.

CodeSprint is a sample student coding challenge designed around teamwork, logical thinking and practical problem-solving. Participants work in small teams and present a simple prototype at the end of the session.

Students from different departments can take part. You do not need to be an expert programmer to join; bring curiosity, a willingness to learn and a laptop if you have one.

  • 10:00 AMCheck-in and team formation
  • 10:30 AMChallenge briefing
  • 11:00 AMBuild session and mentor support
  • 01:00 PMProject demos and closing

Mentor names and profiles will be announced by the organizing team. The sample portal uses this section to show where speaker details will appear.

  • Register using your valid university details.
  • Arrive before the event begins.
  • Work respectfully with your teammates.
  • Use your own work and credit any outside resources.
